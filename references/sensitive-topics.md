# Sensitive topics (body, health, history, religion, politics)

- **Present it as history/science:** tie every norm to the social context of its time; do not promote it.
- **Spell out the harm** when a practice is harmful (corsets, extreme-thinness trends, surgery, lead/mercury cosmetics): red ⚠ label `MS.tag(..., { warn: true })`.
- **End with a responsible, positive message** (diversity, health, respect).
- **Characters always wear period-appropriate, modest clothing.** No nudity, nothing sexualized, even when illustrating body shapes.
- **Minors:** long, covering clothing.
- **Diversity when generalizing:** skin tone, body type, age.
- **Historical events:** cite sources; avoid absolute claims while scholars still disagree ("almost certainly", "according to most researchers").
- **Contested conflicts (e.g. Israel/Palestine):** give the main view of each side and of historians for every disputed point (causes of 1948 displacement, legality of settlements, blockade, casualty counts); attribute figures to their source ("according to Gaza's health ministry, which does not separate civilians from fighters"); mention violence and losses on all sides; no graphic imagery of the dead; prefer neutral wording ("what it called a pre-emptive strike").
